## 5. New source-level correction and caveat from this pass

Read [QA06 log/save/build negative-contract source proof](BASE_LOG_SAVE_BUILD_NEGATIVE_CONTRACTS_2026-11-20_QA06_WIP.md). Direct frozen P4P source reveals:
1. `port/main.c::log_drain` advances log tail unconditionally after `void log_commit`, whose PSP `sceIoWrite` results are unchecked. **Binary Flight Recorder may not reuse text-log ACK semantics**; silent lost text logs possible when storage fails.
2. `port/paths.c` sets save directory to per-executable `p4_game_dir/saves` and other asset paths also per-install.
