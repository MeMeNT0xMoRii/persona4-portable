- **Mandatory safety remains mandatory:** reviewer recommends no hardware promotion on unresolved GE memory/fence/liveness. Do **not** weaken P0 to meet an artificial ship deadline.
- **Term mismatch:** “SAFE”, “healthy 2-bank”, “significantly faster”, “reproducible” have incomplete thresholds. Require actual before/after accepted-present p50/p95/p99, observed GE highwater/retirement and explicit uncertainty. No numeric user device FPS inferred from mock tests.

It remains a strictly checked first-run C objective, otherwise C blocked; actual linker/heap/device gates remain.
