**Distinct source observation:** the pipeline loop waits while `shown_seq < ge_queued`. At >250ms it samples `sceGeListSync(pipe_last_list,1)` and calls `sceGeListUpdateStallAddr(pipe_last_list,pipe_last_end)`. At >1,000,000us it increments `pipe_watchdog_gave_up` and **breaks without proving `shown_seq==ge_queued` or `ge_finished==ge_queued`**. The code then proceeds through `vertex_used=index_used=0`, vblank, `sceGuFinish()` if in frame, `ge_queued++`, `gate_close(ge_queued)`, toggles `pipe_set` and selects `list_store[pipe_set]`. `gate_close` is not itself a proof that a previous bank has retired. The next-bank FREEZE pointer discrepancy is separately SEC-01p; do not count it as new.

**T0 risk / falsifier:** after a 1s GE stall, a producer may resume using a bank/pool still referenced by the GE/flip thread, causing corruption or masking the original hang. Falsify by a trace of each bank's submitted/GE-completed/accepted-present/retired sequence and stall state proving every reuse follows retirement even on timeout. Conversely, a watchdog timeout without reuse or with safe ownership disproves the hazard for that route. This is NOT proof of a real use-after-reuse or display-list overflow.

**Codex isolated diagnostic protocol:** emit bounded nonblocking events `TIMEOUT(seq, shown, finished, GEid, bank, pool, state)`, `SUBMIT`, `GE_DONE`, `PRESENT_ACCEPTED`, `BANK_REUSE`; assert/report only when `BANK_REUSE` precedes proven GE retirement. Preserve separate original game-thread progress and display progress. Test title nonpipeline negative control, Junes F009_004, fog/reinforcement battle, then HOME after save; use controlled PPSSPP T2 and actual PSP Go T3, never intentionally force a power loss or upload private saves. Test both no-stall and synthetic delayed-completion scenarios in host model first. **Do not 'fix' by indefinite blocking or blindly removing the 1s escape**, as either can reintroduce b115 battle deadlock.
