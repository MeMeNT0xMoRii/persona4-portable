**Consequence:** globally entering a manual skill modal at `0x2D/0x46/0x57`, or even immediately on their bit-0x40 branch, is too early: it can appear when Persona level/ownership rejects a fusion or the user later declines the confirmation dialogue. ## Second newly traced boundary: original accident/rare outcome follows YES, not preview

- In normal mode, `0x34` performs `fclRollRareChance` / branch selection; on ordinary outcome it transitions to `f0=0x0D, f1=0xC5`, on rare outcomes to `0x36/0x37` with alternate-result handling. In triangle mode `0x4D` similarly branches to normal `0x0D/0xC5` or rare `0x4F/0x50`. These routines may replace the result or inherit via an alternate record; manual inherited skills must not be silently imposed on accident results.
- Multi `0x5C` is different: specific Persona flags can be set before `0x5E` -> `0x0D/0xC5`. Do not assume it shares the normal/triangle rare-chance dispatch or the same final hook.
- Opening manual selection *immediately* on YES (the 0x30/0x49/0x5A affirmative branch) prevents NO-dialog false positives but still precedes rare outcome determination. Merely moving the hook from `0x2D` to `0x30` does not solve the accident semantic issue.
