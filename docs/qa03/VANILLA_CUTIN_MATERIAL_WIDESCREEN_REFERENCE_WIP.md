## 1. All-Out isn't a single zoomed portrait; original material groups can be identified

- Izanagi [`docs/renderer_port.md` L929–948](https://github.com/Raikaru/Izanagi/blob/bfb12b6896955b626cc45254c92e8b76789ae147/docs/renderer_port.md#L929-L948) identifies battle cut-in **ordering table queue `0x00795050`** and distinct generic cinematic queues `0x00796520..0x00796640`. The All-Out is **layered screen-model geometry**: orange `haikei_koma` panels, black `koma_moyou` / `haikei_moyou_kuro` patterns, white `siro_line` dividers, different portraits, central exclamation and sparkle/glints. Their geometry and texture sampling must not all follow the same 16:9 rule.
- The PC implementation [`src/render/p4gl_im2d.c` L1052–1110](https://github.com/Raikaru/Izanagi/blob/bfb12b6896955b626cc45254c92e8b76789ae147/src/render/p4gl_im2d.c#L1052-L1110) **classifies names within battle model scope**:
  - `bord`, `back`, `flash`, `kasan01`, `kasan02`: **horizontal fill only** (preserve source Y/strip aperture).
  - `haikei_*`, `koma_moyou`, `siro_line`, and qualified `koma_flash_{a..d}`: **uniform cover**, keeping panel seams/dividers aligned.
  - `koma_{a..d}`: **polygon-masked portrait parts**, not rectangular full images; widen/position hull while counter-scaling sampler UV to **preserve face proportions** and avoid opposite-edge sampling, with different side/top/bottom anchor rules.
  - `megane_kiran_{a..d}`: **glints paired to portraits**, translated with corresponding layer; icons/exclamation and unrelated circular UI retain original centered fitted proportions.
- Izanagi [`docs/renderer_port.md` L970–1027](https://github.com/Raikaru/Izanagi/blob/bfb12b6896955b626cc45254c92e8b76789ae147/docs/renderer_port.md#L970-L1027) explicitly tests the All-Out intro/backing, group panels and eyes separately, and describes `koma_a..d` clipping/UV at the wide edge. The same doc says `SYKN_1..8`, `SYKN_E109`, `SYKN_E112` share backgrounds/eye layer roles. **Not a guarantee that Golden uses those exact Vanilla assets.**
- **Important original source bridge:** pinned PS2 [`src/Graphics/Model/mdlManager.c` `mdlGetFrameNameUserData` **FUN_00474CE0** L2791](https://github.com/Raikaru/Persona4-Decompilation/blob/363eab8a966d60a9773b45f330f2344329ec4f0a/src/Graphics/Model/mdlManager.c#L2791-L2820) reads a user-data name string from a RenderWare frame/material. Pinned [`mdlManager.c` L4180–4190](https://github.com/Raikaru/Persona4-Decompilation/blob/363eab8a966d60a9773b45f330f2344329ec4f0a/src/Graphics/Model/mdlManager.c#L4180-L4190) uses the getter to compare material names against animation/color records. Thus **material name is a real game-data property**, not a heuristic invented from pixels. **Do not dereference this on the PSP without verifying object type, pointer validity and that a named user-data entry exists.**

## 2. Original scene owner and why earlier sprite hypotheses were insufficient

- Existing P4P QA03 pinned trace: `btlActionRoundupMesUpdate` FUN_001AE800 → `btlCutinLoad` FUN_001F9CF0 / `btlCutinUpdate` FUN_001FA190 → original `mdlEffectFlushTreeFrame` FUN_00485870 per-child `D_00713480 + kind*0x40` callback. The exact primitive kind for #112/#118/#119, its exact PSP draw route and actual crop still **T0**.
- The independent PC queue `0x795050` gives a **highly selective runtime filter** when an approved PSP diagnostics build is available: log only this queue's effect draws / material user-data names and source+output hull/UV/scissor, rather than all `sdkSpriteDraw` or first 64 generic Im2D calls. **Queue producer/consumer hooks in the PSP build are not yet proven**, so verify against an actual scene and no wild pointer deref.
- **No universal magic scale**: to fix portrait crop, increasing one image quad can make heads *more* cropped. Panels/black/white separators need structural cover while `koma_*{a..d}` texture coords counter-transform and edge anchors preserve original Vanilla face. Two/three/four participants can have different steep diagonal masks; at least **two animation frames per participant-count variant** are required for T2. The original PS2 4:3 and the Golden Vita-wide sample should be retained as **separate** references, not forced pixel-identical.

## 4. Additional original UI geometry concepts worth selectively borrowing

- Izanagi [`src/render/p4gl_im2d.c` L1208–1300](https://github.com/Raikaru/Izanagi/blob/bfb12b6896955b626cc45254c92e8b76789ae147/src/render/p4gl_im2d.c#L1208-L1300) scopes layout by **ordering-table queue/callback**, not by superficial raster bounds. `0x00122A40` field calendar/date, `0x002167F0` party portrait roster and others are right-anchored; battle panels `0x0020B1A0..0x0020B5B0` get left layout; effect/population viewport fills remain full-width. This independently supports our **class-local UI-A/B/C strategy**.
- However, the actual yellow **lower-left command wheel** was already correctly identified as **`btlSelectionDrawCommands` FUN_00207B00**, not `FUN_0020B1A0` central panel. **Do not blindly transplant Izanagi's callback-range code to move that wheel**. Its `shdDrawRadialFan` yscale/safe-area remains a separate local test.
- Izanagi [`src/Main/Social/gc_model.c` `pscBuildModelMatrix` FUN_0036ABD0](https://github.com/Raikaru/Persona4-Decompilation/blob/363eab8a966d60a9773b45f330f2344329ec4f0a/src/Main/Social/gc_model.c#L72-L110) (original source) uses **physical viewport width/height and camera X/Y view window** when scaling decorative PSC panel Y. Another reason not to globally force equal 2D X/Y in the PSP renderer. The PC study explicitly distinguishes PSC art and world/effect camera.
- **Title geometry**: Izanagi stretches the **single background photograph by uniform cover**, extends solid black aperture bars horizontally but keeps logo/text fitted; this is **independent of movie-fit modes**. This may inform our title Widescreen polish but **does not explain or resolve** the separate flashes and poor title-transition FPS.
