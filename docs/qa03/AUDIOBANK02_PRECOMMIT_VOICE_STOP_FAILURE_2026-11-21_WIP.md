This is a separate temporal hazard: already playing old-bank cues can be interrupted even when the requested new bank never commits. Whether the original game's bank-change semantics intentionally stop existing cues at request time is unknown.

**Hypothesis/falsifier:** Replacement request disrupts active SFX and later fails, yielding silence or cue truncation; falsify by original PS2 caller semantics proving immediate stop intentional, or by T2 showing no active sequence/voice during real bank transitions. Do not change to atomic bank swap without checking SAS voice pointers: voices may reference freed old `BD` memory after a successful replacement. The correct policy may require intentional stop on *successful commit* but early stop on some explicit resets.
